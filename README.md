## ⚠️ Scientific Integrity & Prototype Disclaimer
**AstroHelix** presented in this repository is an **Interactive Working Prototype Simulation & Proof-of-Concept**. 
- **Model Validation:** Predictions, indices, and trend plots are illustrative simulations designed to demonstrate system architecture.
- **Experimental Status:** Comparative resilience metrics and biological countermeasures represent conceptual models and hypotheses rather than validated clinical measurements.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
Deep-space exploration exposes astronauts to continuous Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) and microgravity, leading to potential central nervous system degradation and cellular stress. **AstroHelix** is a conceptual framework designed to integrate open-access space biological research data into an intuitive, multi-layered bio-resilience pipeline for risk visualization and strategy simulation.

---

## 🔄 How It Works: The 9-Step Integrated Pipeline

The framework links biological data inputs to prospective risk mitigation strategies through a structured 9-step workflow:

1. **NASA Historical Data:** Ingests research references from NASA OSDR (e.g., OSD-611, OSD-514) on spaceflight stress responses.
2. **Early Biological Signals:** Identifies gene expression pattern changes and cellular stress markers.
3. **Neuronal Vulnerability Index:** Calculates model-estimated neural stress levels based on simulated cosmic radiation exposure.
4. **Comparative Resilience:** Cross-maps human physiological vulnerabilities against resilient organisms (e.g., Tardigrade extremophile adaptation models).
5. **Biological Research Targets:** Highlights prospective genetic and biomolecular repair pathways (e.g., `BRCA1`, `TP53`, `BDNF`, `IL-6`, `APOE`).
6. **Three Adaptive Strategies:** Combines Environmental shielding, Biological repair, and Personalized adjustments into unified countermeasure recommendations.
7. **Bio-Temporal Simulation:** Models progressive neural risk trends over an illustrative 180-day mission timeline.
8. **What-If Universe:** Simulates comparative hypothetical scenarios (e.g., baseline radiation vs. shielded countermeasure protection).
9. **Evidence Assessment Meter:** Documents model validation confidence transparently for academic and judging evaluation.
